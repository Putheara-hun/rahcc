Confirm before construction starts

  • Structural sign-off. Member sizes and reinforcement here are preliminary and typical for a two-storey RC frame of these spans. A licensed structural engineer must check and sign them before you pour concrete.
  • Soil test. The footings assume a safe bearing capacity of at least 80 kPa at 1.5 m depth. On soft clay or filled land (common in low-lying city areas), the engineer may change them to piles.
  • Survey and permit. Have a surveyor confirm the boundaries, road level and north point, then get the construction permit from the local land-management office. Check the setback rules and whether windows are allowed facing the 0.95 m side passage.
  • Party wall. The left wall sits on the boundary. Agree the footing and party-wall details with your neighbour before digging.
All dimensions are in millimetres. Levels are in metres above the road crown at the front boundary (±0.000). Wall positions are measured to the wall centreline and the gridline. Use the written dimensions and never scale from the screen. Where anything disagrees, the written dimension and the engineer's drawings take priority.
