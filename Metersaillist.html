How the quantities are calculated

  • Concrete is the net volume of each element from the structure data. Beam volumes exclude the part inside the slab. The stair is measured as waist, steps and landing.
  • Site-mixed C25 is 1 : 1.5 : 3, which uses about 7.5 bags of cement, 0.45 m³ of sand and 0.85 m³ of gravel per m³. Lean C15 is 1 : 3 : 6. If you buy ready-mix instead, drop the cement, sand and gravel for concrete and price the m³ from the concrete table.
  • Reinforcement uses typical kg/m³ ratios for each element, split by bar size, plus 5% laps and waste. Bars are 12 m long.
  • Brickwork is the net wall area (length × height between slab and beam, minus openings). It assumes 80 × 80 × 180 mm hollow clay bricks at 58 per m² per skin, plus 5% waste.
  • Plaster covers both faces of every wall plus 8% for columns and beams, with 15 mm of 1 : 4 mortar at 0.018 m³ per m². Brick mortar is 0.022 m³ per m² per skin.
  • Tiles include 7% waste, or 10% for small tiles and planks. Roof waterproofing covers the terrace, the stair-house roof and 300 mm upstands, plus 10% for laps.
